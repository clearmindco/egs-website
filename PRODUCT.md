# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Established local home-service business owners. Beachhead market is roofing, followed by HVAC, plumbing, electrical, landscaping, painting, pressure washing, and pools. Roughly 3–50 employees, proven demand, and able to invest in growth. They are still the operating system of the business — leads are missed or answered too slowly, the website undersells the company, marketing/sales/operations run as disconnected efforts, follow-up is inconsistent, and growth creates stress instead of leverage. They want better systems, not more marketing tasks.

## Product Purpose

The site exists to help these owners recognize the hidden systems holding their business back, establish EGS as a trusted growth partner, and move them to request a Business Performance Audit™. It does not exist to sell a website, and it is not a portfolio, service menu, or generic lead-generation funnel.

## Positioning

EGS is a Business Performance Company — a growth partner, not a marketing agency, web design company, SEO agency, lead-generation company, advertising agency, or digital agency. The mechanism a category competitor could not truthfully copy: EGS improves the whole connected system behind growth (the EGS Growth Engine™ — Be Found, Earn Trust, Capture Every Opportunity, Respond First, Win the Job, Create an Amazing Experience, Turn Customers into Promoters, Improve & Grow) rather than selling disconnected marketing tactics.

## Operating Context

Primary entry offer: Business Performance Audit™. At this stage, lead flow is manual: form submissions route to Javier's inbox and get logged in the EGS Opportunities system in Notion. No CRM/automation yet — introduced only once volume or complexity justifies it. Company priorities in order: acquire the first qualified clients, build a repeatable client experience, validate the Audit and core offer, document delivery/sales processes, build proof from real work, then scale.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Stack: Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, hosted on Vercel, repo on GitHub, pnpm. No CMS at this stage. The site is **not deployed publicly** — production launch requires Javier's explicit approval. The production logo is not final; the current mark is a text-based EGS wordmark placeholder that must remain swappable without a layout change.

## Brand Commitments

Name: Elite Growth Strategies (EGS). Promise: "We Build Businesses That Perform Better™." Philosophy: "Better Systems Create Better Businesses™." Personality: calm, confident, precise, disciplined, prepared, honest, strategic, relentless, humble — never loud, pushy, salesy, trendy, cheap, fake-luxury, or full of corporate jargon. Locked visual system: near-black/charcoal base (#0F1115 midnight / #171A21 graphite), white type, a single electric-blue accent (#3B82F6), Fraunces (editorial display) paired with Manrope (body/UI). Design direction is "calm confidence over flashy effects" — editorial-first, oversized typography, architectural whitespace, not a generic SaaS template.

## Evidence on Hand

No clients, testimonials, case studies, or performance statistics exist yet. Do not fabricate any of these under any circumstance — this is an explicit, repeated constraint. Credibility at this stage comes only from founder experience, methodology, and process rigor.

## Product Principles

1. Diagnose before prescribing — lead with the business problem, not a service list.
2. Protect the trusted-advisor tone; never read as a generic agency or lead-gen vendor.
3. Prioritize qualified-owner clarity (getting to an Audit request) over internal polish.
4. Never fabricate proof, clients, or results.
5. Match complexity to company stage — don't build for scale the business doesn't have yet.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No product-specific requirement beyond general WCAG-conscious defaults (contrast, keyboard navigation, visible focus states), already applied in the current build.
